# Free Software & Code — free code from MocGoogle

Every piece of code behind this store, labeled and shelved, free to take and use, same as the books. Free to look, free to carry home.

## The shelf

1. **Deep Cosmic Glow** — `deep-cosmic-glow.css`
   Dark space background, soft blue glow top left and purple glow bottom right, bright readable text, blue links. Pure CSS, no image files, loads fast. Live now on the front page, About, and Questions pages.
2. **Starfield** — `starfield.css`
   Near-black sky with layered white and blue star dots, green links. Held for later, to give a room its own vibe.
3. **Blueprint Grid** — `blueprint-grid.css`
   Dark background with faint cyan grid lines, cyan links. Held for later, to give a room its own vibe. Also used as the floor of the Flashy Wall 3D Room prototype.
4. **Virtual Room Responsive Wrap** — `virtual-room-responsive.css`
   Centers a 3D room, max 1100 wide, perspective that scales with the screen, text that stays tappable on phones without going huge on a computer, and flatter side walls under 600 wide so videos and book spines stay wide enough for a thumb. Applied to the Flashy Wall 3D Room prototype; saved here for the Shelves room and the cubes.
5. **MocGoogle 3D Spinning Box** — `mocgoogle-3d-spinning-box.html`
   The whole 6-sided cube, turning the box instead of scrolling the page. Front True Freedom, Right Shelves and Glass Board, Back Science Podcasts, Left Flashy Wall, Top Sound App, Bottom Dragons, Brainiac and Contact. Swipe, mouse wheel, or Turn and Flip buttons. Live on the store as cube.html (the live copy also carries a meta description and About and Questions links).
6. **Video Theater Frame** — `video-theater-frame.css` + `video-theater-frame.js`
   The custom frame around every YouTube embed on the store. Rounded corners, subtle blue glow border, 16:9 for landscape videos, tall frame for vertical Shorts, with a Share and Copy Link bar underneath. The CSS frames the video, the JS makes the two buttons work. Live on the Flashy Wall and the Shelves since 2026-11-24.
7. **Brainiac Pinball Word Machine** — `brainiac-pinball.html`
   A full pinball game with a 30-position letter ring. Hit letters to fill the rack, the game auto-spells words from its dictionary, and the most words spelled takes the #1 spot on the board. Free to play, free to download. Live on the store as brainiac-pinball.html.
8. **3D Depth Box** — `depth-box.html`
   The depth-approach endless square. Instead of scrolling up and down or turning a cube, each square comes out of the deep background straight toward you as you scroll or swipe forward, then glides past to reveal the next one, looping back to the start. Add as many rooms, books, or videos as you want to the FEED list.
