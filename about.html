About MocGoogle

MocGoogle is a free creative hub made by Richard Latimer, a journeyman plumber and former industrial plant manager from Oswego, New York. He left school after the tenth grade and learned his trade in maintenance bays in the middle of the night, and spent about eight years at a wire mill besides. The books, podcasts, videos, and tools on this site are his own work, written and made in his own words, and given away free.

What moc means

Moc means Mocking Communications. It is the mock of the whole communications establishment, and it is com backwards, the com from commercial. The picture stays the big houses' side. The sound layer, the music and audio people lay under everything and carry from video to video, is the moc's road.

What the store holds

Free books about physics and the universe, original science podcasts, moving pictures, dragons, and a free text-to-speech Sound App. Everything is free to look at, free to listen to, and free to carry home. No money, no door, no login.

Where to find him

Richard Latimer lives in Oswego, New York. The way to reach him is by email at mocgoogle@mocgoogle.com.
