About MocGoogle.com

MocGoogle.com is an independent digital library, creative workshop, and open media hub built on a simple belief: knowledge and creativity should be free for everyone. Under the banner of True Freedom, visitors can read books, listen to podcasts, watch videos, and use browser tools without subscriptions, logins, or paywalls.

Who Created MocGoogle?

MocGoogle was created by Rick Latimer, an independent thinker and author based in Oswego, New York. Rather than locking ideas behind costly memberships or ads, Rick built MocGoogle to share original writing, alternative science discussions, and practical tools directly with curious minds everywhere.

The site is kept running with technical setup and web support from Mouse, page hosting through GitHub, and video hosting on YouTube.

What You Will Find Here

  • The Glass Board & The Shelves: Complete original books and manuscripts kept in the open for free reading.
  • Science Podcasts: Direct-play audio episodes exploring physics, energy, light, gravity, and fresh ways of looking at the universe.
  • The Flashy Wall: Original videos, visual experiments, and guest features.
  • The Sound App: A free text-to-speech browser tool that turns your written words into spoken audio.
  • Dragons: Imaginative family storytelling and creative learning concepts.
