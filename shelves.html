The Shelves

While you are here, the rest of the store is standing on the same shelves. Physics in Alien Space is my book, built chapter by chapter out of a lifetime of looking at the world from the center and the outside at the same time, and Mocgoogle and Mocphysics are its sister titles. The Brainiac Circle is my way of marrying math and English into one small ring of sticks that a brain can read at a glance, anchors first and the rooms between filled in by the reader, the way a whole page is read instead of one line. The song and the videos for mocing Google through its own back door start here too, because attention with no address evaporates, and this is the address.

This store stands on free shelves: mocgoogle.com gives the works away, GitHub holds the pages, Netlify keeps the door open, and Muse minds the counter.
