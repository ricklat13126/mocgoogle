Questions & Answers

What is MocGoogle?

MocGoogle is a free creative hub made by Richard Latimer. It holds his free books, original science podcasts, moving pictures, dragons, and a free text-to-speech Sound App.

Is everything really free?

Yes. Everything on this site is 100 percent free. There are no subscriptions, no paywalls, no hidden fees, and no toll at the door. Free to look, free to listen, and free to carry home.

Do I need an account or a login?

No. There is nothing to sign up for and no login required. Open a page, read a book, or push play on a podcast.

What does the Sound App do?

The Sound App reads text aloud. Pick a tone, give it the words, and hear them spoken. It is free, and it runs in your browser on your phone or computer.

What are the podcasts about?

The podcasts are original science talks about the universe, energy, gravity, light, and how reality really works, made to spark fresh thinking and offer a second opinion on traditional science. You can stream every episode right from your browser.

What does moc mean?

Moc means Mocking Communications, the mock of the whole communications establishment, and it is com backwards. The sound layer is the moc's road.

Can I share the books, podcasts, and videos?

Yes. Everything here is free to carry home and pass along. Every video carries mocgoogle.com on it, so the store travels with the thing itself.

Who made this site?

Richard Latimer of Oswego, New York, a journeyman plumber and former plant manager who writes about physics and the universe in his own words. You can read more on the About page.

How do I get in touch?

Write to mocgoogle@mocgoogle.com. There is also an alternate address, mocingoogle@mocingoogle.com.
