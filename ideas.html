The World Link

I want to unify the world, and the way to do it is not to make everyone the same. Everyone keeps their own, and yet we are all still connected. That is the whole trick.

Most countries do not use .com, they use their own endings, and that is fine, because the moc is not an ending, it is the link. The same moc word, carried into each country's own ending, so a visitor anywhere meets the same moc wearing their own letters. One link, every country, everyone keeping their own.

Dated 2026-10-24, because this is a good one. Richard Latimer, Brainiac.
