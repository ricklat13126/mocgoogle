Bring Me a Problem

Folks around here call me Brainiac. I spent my life figuring things out with my hands, and this store is my counter moved onto the internet. The whole store stands on this one page, in front of you at a glance, every room behind its own doorway. Pick a door.

The Counter
Bring me a problem. The first look is free, small problems stay free, and what gets figured out gets posted with the date on it.
 The Glass Board
Every number this store has, counted in the open: visits, money in, money out, and the disaster fund when it opens. No second set of books.
 The Flashy Wall
Moving pictures to slow a walking man down: my Sister in orbit, her web around the Earth, and guest doorways to my friends' music.
 The Shelves
The book, Physics in Alien Space, its sister titles Mocgoogle and Mocphysics, the Brainiac Circle, and the songs, all standing in one place.
The Supporters' Board

This wall is reserved for the folks whose notices help keep the counter free. Plainly labeled, always, and never inside an answer. The first names go up when the store opens.
