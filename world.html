The World Link

Everyone keeps their own, yet we are all still connected

I want to unify the world, and the way to do it is not to make everyone the same. Everyone keeps their own, and yet we are all still connected. That is the whole trick.

Most countries do not use .com, they use their own endings, and that is fine, because the moc is not an ending, it is the link. The same moc word, carried into each country's own ending, so a visitor anywhere meets the same moc wearing their own letters. The moc stays the same, the ending changes with the country, and the link holds across all of them.

It is a bridge in front, and a bridge between countries, and what crosses it is the sound layer and the music, because music crosses borders without needing a translation. One link, every country, everyone keeping their own.

Most plans for unifying the world ask everyone to give up their own and become the same. This one does the opposite. Nobody gives up anything, and the moc is the thread that ties them together without pulling them out of shape. Unity without uniformity, connection without conquest.

Dated 2026-10-24, because this is a good one. Richard Latimer, Brainiac.
