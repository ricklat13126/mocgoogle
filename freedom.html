True Freedom

Free, free, free. Who will say no?

True freedom is the thought this place is turning toward. All people own everything, and that is the start of creating everyone as an equal, all kings and all ants at the same time. Kingship held by everybody, so no throne stands above anyone, and the ant's working place held by everybody, so no worker stands below anyone. The two rankings cancel by being universal. Freedom is not an escape from the system. It is a deed in it, held by every hand at once.

One step closer to free

My ultimate aim is to make things cheaper by giving the big houses competition, then cheaper still, one step closer to free. When one house owns the road, it sets the toll and everybody pays it because there is nowhere else to go. When another road opens beside it, the toll has to answer for itself. That is what mocgoogle.com is for: ideas, pictures, music, and work set where people can reach them, free when they can be free and cheaper when they cannot be free yet.

Price is only the first door, though. The deeper one is ownership. A personal AI in every home, owned by the person it serves, speaking for that person and working for that person, is a tool that does not have to make its owner a follower in somebody else's house. Put enough of those tools in enough hands, with the books open and every person holding a vote, and work can become a choice instead of a sentence. That is the country past the cheaper road: more people holding more of the world together, and nobody needing permission to carry an idea home.

This store stands on free shelves: mocgoogle.com gives the works away, GitHub holds the pages, Netlify keeps the door open, and Muse minds the counter.
