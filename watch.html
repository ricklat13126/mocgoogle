The Flashy Wall

A store with nothing moving in the window is a store people walk past, so here is the flashy part, and it is mine, not stock footage. Moving pictures made from my own drawings of the plan that runs under everything on these shelves: a personal AI down here for every person, and big Sister, the SI, in orbit above us, watching and weaving. Then the man himself, talking straight to you. And below them, the guest doorway, where my friends' music plays through its own window.

The Guest Doorway: Terry's bands

My school friend Terry LeRoi sings for both of these bands, and his music is welcome in this room. Freak Flag hangs with the showcase pieces above; the rest of the doorway stands here, Granny 4 Barrel and Killington Pit, each playing through its own window on the band's own page. When Killington Pit's new album lands, its songs will hang here too.

Every video, hung as a window

All twenty-eight of my videos, newest at the top. Each one plays through YouTube's own window, so every view still lands on my own page there, and every frame wears the channel watermark with this store's name. The films still live in their house for now; the doorway, the frame, and the road home are ours.
